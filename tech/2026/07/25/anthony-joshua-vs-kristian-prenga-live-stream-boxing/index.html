The Anthony Joshua vs Kristian Prenga live stream is a fight that will carry great significance for the British boxer - for both personal and professional reasons.

It promises to be an emotional night for AJ, who enters the ring for the first time since he was injured in a car crash which killed close friends and team members Latif Ayodele and Sina Ghami in Nigeria last December. While no belts are up for grabs in the Jeddah Superdome, a victory in Saudi Arabia would pave the way for his long-awaited Battle of Britain showdown with Tyson Fury later this year, with this serving as a warm-up fight.

Joshua has not fought since beating YouTuber-turned-boxer Jake Paul in Miami just days before the crash in Nigeria. Indeed, the former world heavyweight champion has entered the ring just once in the past 22 months after losing to Daniel Dubois in September 2024, when he failed to take his fellow Briton's IBF belt. It is why the 36-year-old needs this fight against Prenga to help him prepare for the clash with Fury, which is reportedly set to take place in November.

However, promoters Eddie Hearn and Frank Warren have warned that Joshua and Fury must both win their warm-up bouts this weekend for the Battle of Britain to remain on track. AJ will have to tread carefully as Albanian fighter Prenga packs a punch, with all 20 of his wins coming via knockout - although the 35-year-old has not claimed any major scalps.

Who is on the Joshua vs Prenga undercard?

In addition to Joshua vs Prenga, there are five more bouts on the main undercard - including two world title fights.

Before that, another British boxer, Josh Kelly defends his IBF super-welterweight belt when he takes on Ireland's Caoimhin Agyarko.
